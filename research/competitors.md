# Innomate: competitor research, keyword map, content and GEO plan

Prepared 2026-09-30 for the Innomate (innomate-tech.com) website rebuild. The new site will be a light theme, Arabic first with English.

## How this was gathered, and what it can't tell you

- Every competitor below was checked live on 2026-09-30. For each one I downloaded the HTML with curl and pulled out the `<title>`, meta description, H1–H3, JSON-LD `@type`s, fonts, the most-used hex colours, hreflang, CMS generator and internal links. I also read some pages through WebFetch and ran web searches (US-based search engine; Arabic queries were run too).
- **Search volumes were not measured.** Ahrefs and Similarweb are available as connectors but aren't authorised in this session. The "Vol." column in the keyword map is my estimate from search behaviour and from how crowded the results were: **H** = likely high, **M** = medium, **L** = long-tail. Before committing, check them in Google Keyword Planner or Ahrefs with location Egypt and language Arabic.
- Visual themes come from CSS tokens and colour frequency in the HTML, not screenshots. Anything I inferred rather than saw is marked *(inferred)*.
- ETA deadlines quoted from third-party sites are marked *(third-party, verify on eta.gov.eg)*.

## 4. Content recommendations per page (to beat what's out there)

**Every service page follows the same skeleton**, so users and AI engines both learn the shape:
1. H1 with the primary keyword + a one-sentence **definition** ("ربط الفاتورة الإلكترونية هو …"). This is the sentence AI tools quote.
2. "لمن هذه الخدمة" (who it's for), including the specific sectors, linked.

4. How it works: 4–7 numbered steps with durations.

## 5. GEO / AI-search tactics

1. **Definitional first sentences.** Every page opens with "X هو …" or "X is …" in 25–40 words that stand alone. AI Overviews and Perplexity quote self-contained sentences. Add one line that ties the brand to the entity: "Innomate شركة مصرية مقرها العاشر من رمضان تطبّق نظام ERPNext وتربطه بمنظومة الفاتورة والإيصال الإلكتروني".
2. **Question-shaped headings + short answers** (iBansa, Frappe and Plementus FAQs). Use H2 = the question as users ask it, then a 40–60 word answer, then detail. Mark up FAQPage on service, sector, pricing and FAQ pages. Only Plementus, Al-Ameen and Frappe do this now.
3. **Comparison and fact tables** in real `<table>` markup: e-invoice vs e-receipt, ERPNext vs Odoo, cloud vs on-premise, package inclusions. LLMs pull from tables readily.
4. **A dated, cited ETA timeline**, with each fact linked to eta.gov.eg, the SDK or incometax.gov.eg, and a visible "آخر تحديث" date. Engines prefer sources that cite the primary authority, and freshness matters for regulation queries.
5. **llms.txt (+ llms-full.txt), modelled on Plementus's,** not an auto-generated link dump. Include a one-paragraph entity summary (legal name, founded, city, services, sectors, languages), **verifiable claims with where to verify them** (e.g. a frappe.io partner listing *if Innomate has one*; the Commercial Registry), and links to the key pages with one-line descriptions, in Arabic and English. Innomate currently returns 404.
6. **Schema, per page type:**
   - Site-wide: `Organization` + `LocalBusiness` (or `ProfessionalService`) with `address` (10th of Ramadan), `geo`, `telephone`, `areaServed: EG`, `knowsLanguage: ar, en`, `sameAs` (LinkedIn, Facebook, Google Business Profile, frappe.io partner page if any, Clutch/GoodFirms if any), `founder`, `foundingDate`. Plus `WebSite`.
   - Service pages: `Service` (`serviceType`, `provider`, `areaServed`, `offers` with a price range where published) + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`.
   - Products and apps (if Innomate packages its ETA or ZKTeco connectors): `SoftwareApplication` with `offers` (Daftra and Al-Ameen do this).
   - Case studies: `Article` (+ `about`, `mentions`) and, where a client consents, `Review` with a named `author`. Don't self-serve `AggregateRating` on your own services (Google ignores self-serving review markup for LocalBusiness/Organization); use it only on a genuine product.

## 6. Theme observations and direction for a light Innomate site

**What competitors do:**
- **Almost all are light themes.** The exceptions are the current Innomate site (dark navy) and LavaLoon's dark-logo variant. The new light direction matches the category.
- **The palettes are all blue.** Data Value `#2563eb`, Daftra `#026bff`, TDS `#1eabe3`, Wafeq indigo `#4f46e5`, Frappe `#0089ff`, Innomate `#2c7be5`. The ones that stand out chose something else: Operion purple `#6d329e`, LavaLoon lava red/amber, Plementus plum-on-ink, Al-Ameen teal.
- **Fonts:** Cairo (TDS, Innomate now), Readex Pro (Data Value), Tajawal (LavaLoon), SST Arabic (Operion), Plus Jakarta Sans (Plementus), Newsreader serif (Frappe). Cairo and Tajawal are everywhere.
- **Motifs:** stock photography (Go-Live, Value), app screenshots and animated invoice mock-ups (Daftra), animated product vignettes (Plementus "This is Odoo, in motion"), gradient-clipped headline text (LavaLoon), doodles (Odoo), mono-font labels (LavaLoon's IBM Plex Mono). Client logo walls are universal.
- **The best-looking:** Plementus (calm ink/grey, one accent, generous whitespace, honest copy) and Frappe (monochrome + serif display = confident). **The weakest:** Value ERP (black-heavy, many fonts), Pioneers (Arial body), Go-Live (stock photos).

**How Innomate can look better (light theme):**
1. **Own a colour that isn't category blue.** For example, keep a deep ink (`#0b1b2b`-ish) for text and pick a distinctive accent (industrial amber, verdigris/teal-green or copper), which suits the factories and 10th-of-Ramadan identity. Use blue only for links. Check contrast (WCAG AA) on light surfaces.
2. **Show real product, not stock photos:** ERPNext screens in Arabic RTL with Egyptian data (EGP, ETA status badges "مقبولة/مرفوضة", a ZKTeco punch log). Nobody shows the ETA status flow visually, so that could be the signature illustration.
3. **Typography:** pair one distinctive Arabic display face with a quiet text face. Avoid defaulting to Cairo/Tajawal like everyone else; consider IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Readex Pro, or a serif Arabic display (e.g. Amiri/Noto Naskh for large headings only) for the Frappe-style "confident" feel. Render "ERPNext", "ZKTeco" and "ETA" as LTR tokens inside RTL text (iBansa does this with a styled span).
4. **Motion with a purpose:** small diagrams that animate the actual flow (invoice → signature → ETA → accepted; fingerprint → check-in → payroll) instead of decorative motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Data-first proof blocks:** a numbers strip, and case cards with one metric each (Plementus- and LavaLoon-style), rather than a logo wall alone.
6. **Industrial sector cards** with real factory photography from Innomate's clients (with permission). This is the strongest way to look different from the SaaS vendors.
7. **Tools as design centrepieces:** the cost estimator and readiness quiz (only Plementus has these, and only in English) as interactive, Arabic-first components.
