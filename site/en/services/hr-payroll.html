An HR and payroll system manages the whole employee cycle (hiring, attendance, leave, pay and deductions) and posts the payroll journal entry automatically into the same accounting system.

Everything here is delivered configured on your data and tested with your team.

    Complete employee file

    Personal data, contracts, documents, grades and job history.

  2. 02

    Attendance and shifts

    Rotating shifts, lateness, early leave and overtime, linked to fingerprint devices.

  3. 03

    Leave and advances

    Leave balances, approval flows, and advances deducted in instalments automatically.

  4. 04

    Payroll run

HR & payroll questions

Question not here?Ask us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply during working hours

Does the system calculate social insurance and income tax?

Yes. We set up the calculation rules under current Egyptian law, check them against your latest payroll before go-live, and update them when the law changes.

Will it work with our existing fingerprint devices?

Most ZKTeco devices connect directly. We check the model and firmware first, and suggest an alternative if needed.

Can each branch have different shifts?

Yes. Shifts are defined per branch, department or employee, including night and rotating shifts.

Can employees see their payslips?

Yes, through the self-service portal on mobile or by email, without access to anyone else’s data.
