What is ERPNext, and does it suit a small company?

ERPNext is an open-source enterprise resource planning (ERP) system that keeps accounting, sales, purchasing, inventory, HR and manufacturing in one database. There is no per-user licence fee, so it works for small and mid-sized companies. We start with the modules you need and add others as you grow.

How long does implementation take?

A standard implementation takes 4 to 12 weeks, depending on the number of branches, modules and how much data has to move. We work in phases, and every phase ends with something running in your company, not with a report.

Do you integrate with Egypt’s e-invoice and e-receipt systems?

Yes, it is one of our core specialisms. We connect ERPNext to the Egyptian Tax Authority’s e-invoice and e-receipt systems: item coding, digital signature, submission, and each document’s status returned inside the system.

Do you train our staff?

Yes. Each team is trained hands-on in the process it runs (accounting, sales, warehouse, HR), in Arabic or English, with illustrated guides the team keeps after go-live.

What support do we get after go-live?

Ongoing support by phone, WhatsApp and email, plus monthly maintenance plans covering updates, backups and improvements. Anything that stops work gets round-the-clock priority.

Which industries do you work with?

Food manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, apparel and textiles, electrical panels, furniture, paints, contracting, education, agriculture and livestock, healthcare, non-profits and professional firms.
